# Mazal Shidduchim
## A Modern Platform for Traditional Jewish Matchmaking

---

## What is Mazal Shidduchim?

Mazal Shidduchim is a dedicated dating mode within the Mazal app designed specifically for Orthodox Jewish singles who prefer the traditional shidduch (matchmaking) process over modern swiping-based dating apps.

We've built the first digital platform that truly respects and enhances the traditional shidduch system, rather than trying to replace it.

---

## The Problem We're Solving

Orthodox Jewish singles face a unique challenge:

- **Mainstream dating apps** don't understand their values, community norms, or the importance of family involvement
- **The traditional shidduch system** works, but it's fragmented - shadchanim work with paper files, communication is scattered across phone calls and texts
- **No central platform** exists where singles, shadchanim, and families can connect efficiently while maintaining the dignity and privacy the process requires

---

## Our Solution

Mazal Shidduchim brings the traditional shidduch process into the digital age while preserving everything that makes it meaningful:

### No Swiping - Only Curated Suggestions

Unlike other dating apps, users don't browse or swipe through profiles. Instead:

1. Singles create a comprehensive "Shidduch Resume"
2. They connect with verified shadchanim (matchmakers)
3. Shadchanim review profiles and send curated suggestions
4. Each suggestion comes with the shadchan's reasoning for why this match could work

This preserves the thoughtfulness and intentionality of traditional matchmaking.

### The Shidduch Resume

We've digitized the traditional shidduch resume with an 8-step profile builder that captures:

**Personal Information**
- Name, Hebrew name, age
- Current location

**Family Background (Yichus)**
- Parents' names, occupations, and origins
- Siblings and birth order
- Grandparents
- Notable rabbanim in the family
- Family minhagim (customs)

**Education & Career**
- Schools attended (elementary through seminary/yeshiva)
- College/university and degrees
- Current occupation

**Religious Outlook (Hashkafa)**
- Community affiliation (Modern Orthodox, Yeshivish, Chassidish, Sephardic, Chabad, etc.)
- For men: minyan attendance, learning schedule, kollel plans
- Personal hashkafa description

**What They're Looking For**
- Description of their ideal match
- Age range preferences
- Which communities they're open to
- Marriage timeline
- Preferences for spouse's learning/working balance

**References**
- Minimum 2 references required
- Types: Rabbi, Teacher, Family Friend, Employer, etc.
- Contact information and preferred contact method

**Photos**
- Optional based on community norms
- Privacy controls (who can see them)

## Key Features

### 1. Shadchan Directory

A searchable directory of verified matchmakers, including:
- Their specialties (young professionals, baalei teshuva, second marriages, etc.)
- Communities they serve
- Languages spoken
- Fee structures
- Success rates and reviews

Singles can connect with relevant shadchanim directly through the app.

### 2. Family Portal

Parents and family members can be involved in the process - just like in real life:

- View suggestions their child receives
- Research potential matches
- Provide feedback and approval
- Receive notifications about new suggestions

The single controls exactly what permissions their family members have.

### 3. Community-Specific Settings

Different communities have different norms. We've built customizable rules:

| Community | Photos | Direct Messaging | Shadchan Required | Parent Approval |
|-----------|--------|------------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Modern Orthodox | Required | Yes | No | No |
| Yeshivish | Optional | No | Yes | Yes |
| Chassidish | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Sephardic | Required | Yes | No | No |
| Chabad | Optional | Yes | No | No |

### 4. Shabbat Mode

The app automatically pauses during Shabbat and Yom Tov:

- Detects candle lighting and havdalah times based on location
- Displays a beautiful "Shabbat Shalom" screen with zmanim
- Automatically resumes after Shabbat
- Configurable timing (e.g., 18 minutes before candle lighting)

This shows respect for the sanctity of Shabbat while ensuring users don't miss anything important.

### 5. Privacy First

We understand that privacy is paramount in the shidduch process:

- Photos can be hidden until you approve a specific suggestion
- Sensitive information (like health details) is only visible to your assigned shadchan
- References are only contacted after mutual interest
- Profile visibility controls

## How It Works

### For Singles

1. **Create Your Profile** - Complete the 8-step Shidduch Resume builder
2. **Connect with Shadchanim** - Browse the directory and connect with relevant matchmakers
3. **Receive Suggestions** - Get curated matches with the shadchan's reasoning
4. **Respond** - Choose "Interested," "Need to Think," or "Not for Me"
5. **Research** - If both sides are interested, you can research further
6. **Connect** - Contact information is shared, and the shadchan facilitates the first date
7. **Track Progress** - The app helps track dating progress through engagement

### For Shadchanim

1. **Get Verified** - Apply to become a verified shadchan on the platform
2. **Build Your Profile** - List your specialties, communities served, and fee structure
3. **Connect with Singles** - Accept connection requests from relevant singles
4. **Make Suggestions** - Browse your connected singles and create thoughtful matches
5. **Track Success** - See the status of all your suggestions and track outcomes

### For Family Members

1. **Get Invited** - Receive an invite from your child/relative
2. **Create Account** - Simple signup process
3. **View Suggestions** - See what's been suggested (if permitted)
4. **Participate** - Provide feedback, approve suggestions, or suggest matches yourself
